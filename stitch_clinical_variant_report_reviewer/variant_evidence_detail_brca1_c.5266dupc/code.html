The insertion of a single cytosine at position 5266 (c.5266dupC) alters the open reading frame starting at residue 1756, introducing a premature stop codon 74 amino acids downstream (p.Gln1756Profs*74). This event is located within exon 20 of the standard 24-exon canonical transcript (NM_007294.4), targeting the critical C-terminal BRCT repeat domain.

Loss-of-function (LoF) is an established disease mechanism for hereditary breast and ovarian cancer predisposition mediated by heterozygous BRCA1 alterations. Because the premature termination codon resides within the final 50 nucleotides of the penultimate exon, transcripts are predicted either to undergo incomplete nonsense-mediated mRNA decay (NMD) or yield an unstable truncated peptide lacking critical tandem phosphopeptide recognition modules. Criterion PVS1 is asserted at full strength under ClinGen sequence variant interpretation (SVI) recommendations.

Historical ClinVar archives reflect 68 concordant diagnostic submissions designating Pathogenicity without conflicting classifications of uncertain significance (VUS). The ENIGMA Expert Curation Panel evaluated this variant (VCV000017677) and granted four-star authoritative status. Subpopulation analysis indicates an enriched founder frequency in individuals of Ashkenazi Jewish heritage, with an odds ratio > 28.4 across multiple controlled cohort studies, robustly meeting criterion PS4.
